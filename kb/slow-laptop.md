---
id: slow-laptop
title: Laptop is slow to boot or fans are loud
team: IT Helpdesk
---
A laptop that takes very long to boot or has loud fans usually has too many startup programs or low disk space.
Restart the laptop, because uptime of many days causes slowdowns.
Open Task Manager and disable unneeded startup apps. Free at least 15 percent of disk space.
Install pending operating system updates.
If the problem remains, the IT Helpdesk can run diagnostics or schedule a hardware check.
