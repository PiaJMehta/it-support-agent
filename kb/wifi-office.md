id: wifi-office
title: Office Wi-Fi is slow or drops
team: Network
---
For slow or unstable office Wi-Fi, note the floor, room and time of day.
Forget the network and reconnect to the corporate SSID, not the guest network.
Check whether other people in the same room have the same problem, which points to an access point issue.
Report the access point location to the Network team so they can check signal strength and channel load.
