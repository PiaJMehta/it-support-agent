---
id: crm-login-error
title: CRM or sales dashboard shows session expired error
team: Enterprise Apps
---
A session expired error in the CRM or sales dashboard is usually caused by stale browser cookies or a single sign-on token problem.
Sign out of the CRM, clear cookies for the CRM site, and sign in again through the company single sign-on page.
Try a private browser window to rule out extensions.
If the error returns, the Enterprise Apps team checks the user's licence and SSO mapping.
