title: Microphone or audio not working in video calls
team: IT Helpdesk
---
If the microphone does not work in video calls, check that the right input device is selected in the call app settings.
Check the operating system privacy settings and allow microphone access for the app.
Unplug and replug a headset, or restart the call app.
If the user has a call soon, offer to join from the phone while the IT Helpdesk investigates the device.
