---
id: password-reset
title: Forgot password or locked out of account
team: Identity & Access
---
If a user forgot their password or is locked out after too many attempts, use the self-service password reset page.
The reset needs MFA approval on the registered phone.
Accounts unlock automatically after 30 minutes. A locked laptop login may need a connection to the office network or VPN to sync the new password.
If self-service reset fails, the Identity and Access team verifies the user and resets the account.
Never ask the user to send their password in a ticket.
