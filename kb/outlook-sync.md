---
id: outlook-sync
title: Email or calendar not syncing to phone or Outlook
team: Enterprise Apps
---
If Outlook email or calendar invites are not syncing, check that the mobile mail app is signed in with the corporate account.
Remove the account from the phone and add it again.
In Outlook, run Send and Receive and check that the mailbox is not full.
If sync still fails on several devices, the Enterprise Apps team checks the mailbox and sync settings.
