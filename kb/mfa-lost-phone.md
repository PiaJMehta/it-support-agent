---
id: mfa-lost-phone
title: New or lost phone, cannot approve MFA prompts
team: Identity & Access
---
When a user gets a new phone or loses the old one, MFA prompts can no longer be approved and login is blocked.
The Identity and Access team must verify the user's identity, then reset the MFA registration.
After the reset the user registers the authenticator app again on the new phone.
This blocks all sign-ins, so treat it as high priority.
