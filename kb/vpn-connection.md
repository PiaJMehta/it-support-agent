---
id: vpn-connection
title: VPN keeps disconnecting or will not connect
team: Network
---
If the VPN drops every few minutes or refuses to connect, first check that the home internet connection is stable.
Quit the VPN client fully and reopen it, then sign in again with MFA.
Update the VPN client to the latest version from the software portal.
Switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection if possible, because weak Wi-Fi causes repeated disconnects.
If it still drops, collect the client log and the time of the last disconnect for the Network team.
